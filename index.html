ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Understanding the science of the human body.

Welcome to the Department of Physiology at North Bengal Medical College and Hospital. This website presents the department's faculty, academic activities, research interests, facilities and important departmental information.

The department is committed to creating a strong foundation in physiology for medical students while encouraging scientific thinking, clinical correlation and research-oriented learning.
